Qualitative score
Likelihood (1–5) × impact (1–5). Inherent is before controls, residual is with controls as they operate today.
Quantitative model
FAIR-style Monte Carlo: 10,000 simulated years (seed 27001). Each year draws an event rate and per-event losses from PERT distributions of the min / most likely / max estimates, with Poisson event counts. Portfolio figures add the risks year by year, so they keep the shape of the tail.
Reading the numbers
Estimates are calibrated ranges, not measurements. Use the results to compare risks and treatments, not as accounting forecasts.
